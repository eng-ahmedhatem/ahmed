What types of cleaning services do you offer?

Adelaide SuperMaids offers regular house cleaning, custom cleaning, super/spring cleaning, end-of-lease cleaning, carpet cleaning, tile and grout cleaning, upholstery steam cleaning, window cleaning and commercial cleaning.

Can I book a regular weekly, fortnightly or monthly clean?

Yes. Regular cleaning is available on weekly, fortnightly and monthly schedules. You can review the regular house cleaning information before booking.

What if I only need a one-off or customised clean?

Custom cleaning is available for customers who want a one-off service built around their priorities, while Super / Spring Cleaning is designed for a more thorough reset.

Do you offer end-of-lease or vacate cleaning?

Yes. Adelaide SuperMaids offers move-in/move-out and end-of-lease cleaning. You can view the end-of-lease cleaning service for more information.

Are your cleaners insured and checked?

Yes. Adelaide SuperMaids uses relevant police and security checks and is covered by comprehensive public liability insurance.

What happens if I am not satisfied with the clean?

If you are not completely satisfied, contact Adelaide SuperMaids within 48 hours and the team will return to re-clean your home or property under the satisfaction guarantee.

Can I book and pay online?

Yes. Online booking is available 24 hours a day, and customers can pay online or choose cash on the day.

Do you clean offices and commercial properties?

Yes. Commercial cleaning is one of the core services listed by Adelaide SuperMaids. Visit the commercial cleaning page for service information.
